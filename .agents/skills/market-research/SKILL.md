# Market Research

## Method
1. Define market/product question.
2. Identify target users and buying/usage context.
3. Gather primary/authoritative sources first.
4. Separate current facts from forecasts and inference.
5. Record publication/source dates.
6. Analyze market dynamics, customer pain points, substitutes, constraints, opportunities, risks.
7. Connect findings to product implications.

## Output
Write/update `docs/01-research/market-research.md`.
No unsupported market-size numbers.
